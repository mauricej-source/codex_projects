How Lottery Gap Analysis Works

1. Gap Calculation

For each main ball from 1-69 and each Powerball from 1-26, the app scans the draw history starting with the most recent row. The gap is the index of the first draw where that number appears. A smaller gap means the number appeared recently. A larger gap means it has been absent longer.

2. Hot, Warm, and Cold Groups

Main-ball numbers are sorted by gap. The 20 smallest gaps become Hot, the 20 largest gaps become Cold, and the remaining 29 numbers become Warm. Powerballs use an 8 Hot / 10 Warm / 8 Cold split so the middle group remains available for selection.

3. Strategy Families

The generator now supports a broader set of named strategies built from Hot, Warm, and Cold counts. Examples include Balanced, Hot-Heavy, All Hot, Hot Light, Warm Light, Warm Heavy, All Warm, Luke Warm, Warm, Cool, Cold, Colder, and Coldest. Strategy Mix is still a rotation mode and currently cycles through Balanced, Hot-Heavy, and All Hot.

4. Ticket Filters and Powerball Choice

Main-ball tickets are only kept if they have a 3:2 or 2:3 odd/even split and a total sum between 130 and 200. The Powerball is chosen randomly from the Warm Powerball group, which represents the middle band under the current gap model.

5. Strategy Definitions and Draw Labels

Balanced = 2 Hot, 2 Warm, 1 Cold. Hot-Heavy = 4 Hot, 1 Warm. All Hot = 5 Hot. Hot Light = 3 Hot, 2 Warm. Warm Light = 2 Hot, 3 Warm. Warm Heavy = 1 Hot, 4 Warm. All Warm = 5 Warm. Luke Warm = 1 Hot, 2 Warm, 2 Cold. Warm = 1 Hot, 3 Warm, 1 Cold. Cool = 4 Warm, 1 Cold. Cold = 1 Hot, 1 Warm, 3 Cold. Colder = 1 Warm, 4 Cold. Coldest = 5 Cold.

The Draw History panel classifies each past row using the current Hot, Warm, and Cold groupings, then shows the exact pattern beneath the strategy name. If a row does not match one of the named compositions, it is labeled Mixed Draw.
