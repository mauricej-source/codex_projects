Top Job Search APIs
JSearch (OpenWeb Ninja): Offers real-time job listings and salary data, often used on RapidAPI.
Jooble API: A major job aggregator with a dedicated search API.
Findwork: Provides a simple API to access job board data.
Techmap / JobDataFeeds: Aggregates millions of international jobs from Career Pages and Job Boards.
TheirStack: Specializes in accessing job postings from LinkedIn, Indeed, and Glassdoor.
USAJOBS API: Official API for US government job board.
SerpApi (Google Jobs): Allows scraping of Google Jobs search results.

Possible With Partner Approval
-- -----------------------------------------------

Monster: plausible, but it looks employer/partner-oriented rather than public-search-API-oriented. Their employer site explicitly mentions ATS integration and partner paths, which suggests access may be possible through business channels, not a simple self-serve search API. Sources: Monster job site, Monster for Employers, Partner with Monster

ZipRecruiter: same general category. Worth checking if you can get partner access, but not as clean as Dice/USAJOBS from an implementation-risk standpoint. Source: ZipRecruiter Partner Platform

-- -----------------------------------------------
Avoid Unless Scraping Is Acceptable
-- -----------------------------------------------

Built In: I found a consumer-facing job/search site, but no public developer search API. Source: Built In

Nomad Careers: niche remote-job site, but I found no API/docs; the site even exposes a crawler-only prerender entrypoint, which is a sign of JS rendering, not official integration support. Source: Nomad Careers

SimplyHired, Indeed: usually fall into the same bucket unless you have a specific approved relationship.
