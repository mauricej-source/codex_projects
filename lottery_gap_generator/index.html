How Lottery Gap Analysis Works

1. Gap Calculation

For each main ball from 1-69 and each Powerball from 1-26, the app scans the draw history starting with the most recent row. The gap is the index of the first draw where that number appears. A smaller gap means the number appeared recently. A larger gap means it has been absent longer.

2. Hot, Warm, and Cold Groups

Main-ball numbers are sorted by gap. The 20 smallest gaps become Hot, the 20 largest gaps become Cold, and the remaining 29 numbers become Warm. Powerballs use an 8 Hot / 10 Warm / 8 Cold split so the middle group remains available for selection.

3. Ticket Strategy Modes

Balanced uses 2 Hot, 2 Warm, and 1 Cold. Hot-Heavy uses 4 Hot and 1 Warm. All Hot uses 5 Hot numbers. Strategy Mix rotates across those three modes while still honoring the requested ticket count.

4. Ticket Filters and Powerball Choice

Main-ball tickets are only kept if they have a 3:2 or 2:3 odd/even split and a total sum between 130 and 200. The Powerball is chosen randomly from the Warm Powerball group, which represents the middle band under the current gap model.
