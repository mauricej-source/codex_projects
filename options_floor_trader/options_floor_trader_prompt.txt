2. **Automated Strategy Discovery Engine**
   Instead of just manual ticker entry, implement a "Discovery Mode":
   - **Pre-set Universe**: Maintain a list of the top 100 most liquid symbols (ETFs like SPY, QQQ, IWM and high-volume equities like AAPL, TSLA, NVDA).
   - **Scanning Logic**: 
      1. Fetch 'Quotes' for the universe to calculate IV Rank.
      2. Filter for the top 20 symbols with the highest IV Rank (to maximize premium).
      3. For those top 20, fetch the full 'Option Chains'.
   - **The "Top Lists"**: Display the "Top 5, 10, 15, and 20" best potential trades across all strategies, ranked by your 'Mathematical Logic (Math Engine)'.
       this can be a dropdown or textbox with an incrementer widget.

   - **Mathematical Logic (Math Engine)**
       Incorporate the following formulas in the 'engine.py' module:
   
       - **Probability of Profit (POP)**:
         - For Credit Spreads: Use `(1 - (Short Delta * 1.1))` as a conservative estimate of expiration probability.
         - For Debit Spreads: Use `(Long Delta - Short Delta)` to estimate directional success.
       
       - **Expected Value (EV)**:
         - Formula: `(POP * Max_Profit) - ((1 - POP) * Max_Loss)`
         - A trade is only 'HIGH' if the EV is positive (> 0).
       
       - **IV Rank Calculation**:
         - `(Current_IV - 52_Week_Low_IV) / (52_Week_High_IV - 52_Week_Low_IV) * 100`
         - High success requires IV Rank > 50 (selling when fear is expensive).
       
       - **Capital Efficiency (for <$25k accounts)**:
         - `(EV / Buying_Power_Required) * 100`. 
         - Rank trades higher if they offer a better return on the specific collateral held by Schwab.

	- The UI must allow the user to toggle between "Standard Account (+$25k)" and "Swing Account (<$25k)".

    A. Strategy Filters:
       - **For +$25k Accounts**: Bull Put Spreads, Bear Call Spreads, Iron Condors, and The Wheel (Cash-Secured Puts).
       - **For <$25k Accounts**: 30-45 Day Credit Spreads, Swing-Trading Debit Spreads, and Poor Man’s Covered Calls (Diagonals).

    B. Ranking Logic (User-Adjustable Defaults):  Implement a logic engine that ranks trades as HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW success based on the following:

       Standard (+$25k) Logic:**
       - HIGH: POP > 70%, Delta < 0.20, IV Rank > 50%.

       Swing (<$25k) Logic:**

       - MEDIUM: POP 50-65%, 7-14 Days to Expiration, IV Rank 20-40%.
       - LOW: High Gamma risk (expiring in <3 days) or Debit spreads with <40% POP.
